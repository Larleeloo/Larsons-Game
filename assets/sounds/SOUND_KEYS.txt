LARSON'S GAME — SOUND KEYS
==========================

Every sound the game can make, the file to name your audio, and the
sound key behind it, by category (the tabs of Pause → Sounds). Drop an
MP3 (or WAV) at the listed path in this folder and the game plays it from
its next launch. The shared sounds come with placeholders; a body's or an
object's own file, when there is one, plays instead of the shared one.
(held) sounds loop for as long as what they are the sound of lasts; the
rest play once as it starts.
Generated by the game from its catalogue (audio/SoundKeys.java).
