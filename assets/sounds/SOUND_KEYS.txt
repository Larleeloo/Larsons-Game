LARSON'S GAME — SOUND KEYS
==========================

Every sound the game can make, the file to name your audio, and the
sound key behind it. Drop an MP3 (or WAV) at the listed path in this
folder and the game plays it from its next launch. Anything you do
not supply is SILENT. (held) sounds loop for as long as their
animation holds; the rest play once as it starts.
Generated by the game from its catalogue (audio/SoundKeys.java).
